# 4.2.4.1.2 Assign and maintain persistent identifiers of the AIP and its components

## Requirement Text

The repository shall assign and maintain persistent identifiers of the AIP and its components so as to be unique within the context of the repository.

## Evidence Provided

All APTrust Intellectual Objects and their components are assigned persistent identifiers upon creation. These identifiers are maintained in perpetuity and are immutable.

See section [4.2.4](04.02.04-convention-that-generates-persistent-unique-identifiers-for-aips.md) for more information.
